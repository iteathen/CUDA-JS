## Why archived

The prior `STATUS.md` had grown into a long historical evidence narrative and repeated the hardware extension registry's overloaded `no-support` terminology. That wording was valid as a public qualification claim but was later interpreted as architectural rejection, contributing to planning drift around concurrent launch and other capability families.

The historical record is preserved exactly in Git at the revision above. It is not deleted or rewritten retroactively.
